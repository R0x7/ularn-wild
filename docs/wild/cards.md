# Monster Trading Cards

Every monster you kill can drop a collectible card: the first kill of each
species always does, later kills 15% of the time, and any drop is a shiny
**holo** 5% of the time. A popup shows the card as it drops (tap it to see
the card full size).

The album keeps every card you have ever found, across all runs and save
slots. Open it from **Card album** on the start screen or in the in-game
menu: 69 cards (every monster, the Wild Edition oddities and the demons),
unknown ones as "???" silhouettes, an `x/69 collected` count and two
filters that combine: by rarity (common, uncommon, rare, epic for dragons and
bosses, legendary for demons) and by what you have (all cards, found, holo,
not found yet), so "Rare" + "Found" shows just your rare cards and the count
line says how many of them there are. Tap a card to view it full screen; holo
cards shimmer.

Settings → Wild features: untick **Monster trading cards drop from kills** to
turn cards off (no drops, no album entries, no menu items; a saved game also
keeps the flag it was started with), and **Card drop toasts** to keep
collecting without the popups.
